## Detailed Steps

1. **Implement Main Application Shell (`src/App.tsx`)**:
   - Layout: Fullscreen container `h-screen w-screen overflow-hidden bg-[#0b0f17] flex flex-col font-sans select-none`.
   - On Mount: Calls `useWorkspaceStore.getState().init()`.
   - Header Section: Mounts `MetricsStrip.tsx`.
   - Canvas Body: Mounts `PartnershipCanvas.tsx` filling all remaining vertical space (`flex-1 relative`).
   - Floating Overlays:
     - Mounts `ActionToolbar.tsx` positioned top-left/top-center above canvas.
     - Mounts `InspectorDrawer.tsx` positioned on the right side.
   - Global Keyboard Handlers:
     - `Escape`: Deselects active node/branch (`clearSelection()`).
     - `Delete` / `Backspace`: When a branch or non-root placement is selected and focus is outside an input, prompts/deletes the selected element.
   - Offline & Webview Protocol Guarantee: Zero `react-router` or pushState history navigation; safe for Tauri v2 WebView2 and `file://` protocols.

2. **Author Acceptance Verification Suite (`test/phase4.test.ts`)**:
   Implement automated end-to-end checks for all 10 items in Section 12 of the technical specification:
   - **Check 1**: Delhi initial state starts in bottom-to-top layout with Root NGO at bottom ($y \approx 615$), Sports Dev Office at top ($y \approx 12$) with `"Triple-Thread (3)"` badge.
   - **Check 2**: Dept of Social Security white branch renders with cross status (faded gray) and filters cleanly when `showDeclined = false`.
   - **Check 3**: Node drag-and-drop leaves coordinates permanently at drop location with $0\text{px}$ drift and zero snap-back.
   - **Check 4**: Dropping a card onto another nudges the colliding card away with $\ge 24\text{px}$ clearance while keeping the dragged card anchored.
   - **Check 5**: "Tidy Up (Bottom-to-Top)" runs Sugiyama layered layout, ordering all nodes with zero bounding-box overlaps.
   - **Check 6**: Parallel branches (Your NGO $\to$ City Museum; Your NGO $\to$ Agrocell CSR) compute distinct lateral Bezier offsets ($\ge 36\text{px}$ clearance) without colliding pills or buttons.
   - **Check 7**: Clicking Cross on one of the three yellow branches into Sports Dev Office immediately turns line gray, downgrades badge from `"Triple-Thread (3)"` to `"2-Thread"`, and recalculates Top Metrics Strip.
   - **Check 8**: Adding a new organization and connecting a confirmed yellow branch into Sports Dev Office promotes the badge to `"Quad-Thread (4)"`.
   - **Check 9**: Architectural boundaries: Automated grep scan confirms zero `localStorage` calls exist outside `localStorageRepository.ts`, and zero DOM/React imports exist inside `src/engine/*`.
   - **Check 10**: Full roundtrip fidelity: Export JSON $\to$ Reset Demo $\to$ Import JSON restores exact topology, placements, coordinates, tick/cross states, and M% values via `WorkspaceRepository`.
