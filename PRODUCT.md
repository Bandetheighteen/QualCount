# Product Context: QualCount

## Target Audience
- **Primary Users:** Social sector leaders, alliance directors, and program coordinators in India and emerging markets managing complex multi-stakeholder initiatives (e.g. CSR foundation grants, municipal government permissions, cultural institution venues, academic co-hosts).
- **Usage Scene:** Operating from local laptops (Windows, macOS, Linux) and field tablets, often in low-bandwidth or offline environments, reviewing live coalition progress during internal review meetings, board presentations, and donor due diligence audits.

## Product Tone & Voice
- **Tone:** Calm, rigorous, authoritative, archival. QualCount is a working analytical ledger and institutional instrument, not a flashy SaaS startup dashboard. Zero marketing hyperbole, zero empty gamification.
- **Visual Stance:** Editorial "Book & Ledger" aesthetic. Crisp 1px architectural rules, warm archival paper surfaces, high-contrast ink line weights, and monospace tabular indices.

## Core Problem & Value Proposition
- Standard CRMs treat relationship building as a flat sales volume funnel.
- Subjective 1-to-5 partner rating surveys produce arbitrary composite numbers that invite Campbell's Law gaming.
- QualCount directly models alliances as a bottom-to-top directed multiplex network (Green = Financial Funding, Archival Charcoal = Direct Owned Ties, Amber = Third-Party Referrals & Bridges) and derives honest, ratio-scale progress and risk metrics strictly from live graph topology and verifiable institutional facts.

## Non-Negotiable Invariants
1. **Verifiable Observations Only:** No arbitrary subjective scoring. Metrics are rooted in Public Choice Theory (Buchanan-Tullock decision touches, Olson selective incentives, Niskanen bureaucratic incentives, Ostrom credible commitments/durability, Centola-Macy social proof).
2. **Deterministic Risk:** No black-box machine learning for small NGO sample sizes. Layer 1 risk is computed by 6 pure TypeScript mathematical parameters with pinpointed node/edge IDs.
3. **Canvas Spatial Agency:** Zero snap-back card movement during manual repositioning; bottom-to-top layout direction (`UP`); local bounding-box anti-overlap; parallel Bezier edge separation; inline tick/cross edge controls.
4. **Data Sovereignty:** Portable Workspace Folders of plain JSON files (`workspace.json`, `entities.json`, `workbooks/`) that sync safely over Google Drive and local drives without cloud lock-in.
